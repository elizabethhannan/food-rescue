    Feed-A-Friend covers the time families spend waiting on slow government and corporate systems. A family waiting on SNAP asks by text; agents set their level of need, fund it with micro-philanthropy, gift-card money, corporate cash and donor-advised fund grants, and send it through delivery networks that already exist. No warehouse. A person steps in by exception. Same simulated week and clock as the desk.

    Level 1 · Bridge$200Groceries within 72 hours. Qualifies: a SNAP application or recertification is pending.
    Level 2 · Priority$300Groceries within 24 hours. Qualifies: need score 55+, waiting past the 30-day federal standard, a child under 5, or a senior or disabled member.
    Level 3 · Crisis$400Groceries within 8 hours and a person checks in. Qualifies: need score 75+, or no food at home with a child under 5.
    Pet bonus+$100Pet food and supplies for the first pet, +$50 for each more (up to +$200). A sick pet is routed to donated vet care.

To receive support they answer an experience survey honestly, sign an honesty statement and upload the letters they have from their state agency. Agents read, redact and verify; a person reviews anything that disagrees. Verified, consented cases feed the State reports tab.

      How the data is kept clean

      RuleWhat it means
      Honesty statementEvery survey is signed; answers are the family's own account and are kept as given.
      Document checkThe Document reader matches each agency letter to the survey (dates, programs, notice type). Blurry letters get a request for a clearer photo; disagreements go to a person.
      RedactionCase numbers, names, addresses and ID numbers are removed from letters before any agent or report sees them.
      ConsentOnly cases with consent to anonymized use are counted. Declining never changes support.
      Small numbersAny state or measure with fewer than 10 cases is suppressed so no family can be identified.
      Required fieldsThe survey cannot be submitted with a required answer missing, so every counted case is complete.
      Human reviewNo report goes to an agency until a person approves it.

Each agent has a contract: what it may do on its own, when it must hand the decision to a person, and the messages it sends. The thresholds are the line between the two.

Agents and what they may do

AgentDoes on its ownHands to a person whenMessages it sends

Message names follow the task pattern used by agent-to-agent protocols such as A2A. In this prototype every agent is simulated in your browser.

Built by Elizabeth Hannan with HXO. HXO (Human Experience Optimization) is a design methodology that starts from human outcomes, not features, technology or business requirements, and tests every system with one question: does it help or hurt the full spectrum of human experience?

Self-initiated concept for Feed-A-Friend, a food rescue nonprofit. Donors, partners, drivers and every number are simulated; nothing here contacts a real person or organization, and nothing you do leaves your browser.

Feed-A-Friend covers the time families spend waiting on slow government and corporate systems. A family waiting on SNAP asks by text; agents set their level of need, fund it with micro-philanthropy, gift-card money, corporate cash and donor-advised fund grants, and send it through delivery networks that already exist. No warehouse. A person steps in by exception. Same simulated week and clock as the desk.

Level 1 · Bridge$200Groceries within 72 hours. Qualifies: a SNAP application or recertification is pending.
Level 2 · Priority$300Groceries within 24 hours. Qualifies: need score 55+, waiting past the 30-day federal standard, a child under 5, or a senior or disabled member.
Level 3 · Crisis$400Groceries within 8 hours and a person checks in. Qualifies: need score 75+, or no food at home with a child under 5.
Pet bonus+$100Pet food and supplies for the first pet, +$50 for each more (up to +$200). A sick pet is routed to donated vet care.

State benefits link Live

With each family's consent, the Benefits link agent checks with the state agency that runs SNAP. When the state confirms benefits, the family's current Feed-A-Friend order is flagged as the last purchase this month, and the bridge closes.
